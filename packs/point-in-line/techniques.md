Fencing has no official technique video series, so these link to lessons by Coach Tyler on
[Fencer's Edge](https://www.youtube.com/@FencersEdge), which covers all three weapons. Where a lesson
is taught for foil, the entry says what changes in épée.

## Flèche {#fleche}
@also flèches, fleches

The running attack: the fencer launches off the front leg and crosses the back leg past it, arm
extended. In épée the touch only counts if it lands before the attacker runs past.

- [How to flèche · épée](https://youtu.be/771TCmbu2eA)

## Lunge {#lunge}
@also lunges

The basic attack: the back leg drives, the front foot kicks out and the arm reaches first. Recovering
fast matters as much as the hit.

- [How to lunge](https://youtu.be/VYBuJljCeqk)
- [Five exercises for a better lunge](https://youtu.be/zseq3eCf_9g)

## Retreat {#retreat}
@also retreats, retreating, step back

Stepping back, front foot first, to keep distance. Retreating quickly is a defence of its own;
retreating by reflex walks you off the end of the piste.

- [How to retreat faster](https://youtu.be/x4JsOGOtCJg)

## Counterattack {#counterattack}
@also counterattacks, counterattacking, counter-attack, stop-hit, stop-hits

Hitting into the opponent's attack instead of parrying it, most often a stop-hit on the advancing arm.
This lesson is for foil, where right of way decides who scores. Épée has no right of way: the first hit
by a twenty-fifth of a second scores, and if both land within that, it's a double.

- [How to counterattack · foil](https://youtu.be/Ez5UKuvroos)

## Distance {#distance}
@also distance control

Staying just out of the opponent's reach while keeping yours ready. Against a fast attacker, distance
is often a better parry than the blade.

- [Distance control drills](https://youtu.be/NdILOISyOx0)
- [Distance control at home](https://youtu.be/8Uqi5Z2yaHk)

## Feint {#feint}
@also feints

A threat meant to draw a reaction: a parry, a retreat or a counter. The real action goes where the
reaction leaves an opening.

- [Feinting footwork](https://youtu.be/slBfeGNKmoM)

## Fencing a left-hander {#left-hander}
@also left-handed, left-hander, left-handers

Left-handers face right-handers every day; right-handers rarely face them. Their open lines are on the
other side, and the attacks you drill land differently.

- [Tips for fencing left-handers](https://youtu.be/9HrD-vKyTyU)
