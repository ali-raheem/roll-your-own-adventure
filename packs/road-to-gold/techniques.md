Many jiu-jitsu techniques are judo techniques under another name. Those link to their page on the
[Judo technique library](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza) and to the matching video from the
[Kodokan's official waza series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_GxcFx8LZRk&list=PLtz539PTepc16H2iu5F3Q3D7_He1EYlIQ).
Techniques that only exist in jiu-jitsu link to lessons on
[Bernardo Faria's channel](https://www.youtube.com/@BernardoFariaBJJ), from Bernardo himself and the
world-class guests he films.

## Seoi nage {#seoi-nage}
@also shoulder throw

The shoulder throw. The thrower turns in, gets the elbow under your armpit and carries you over their
back. Darek's version comes off a collar grip he pumps to break your posture forward.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/seoi-nage)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/zIq0xI0ogxk)

## Seoi otoshi {#seoi-otoshi}
@also drop seoi, drop seoi nage, drop shoulder throw

The drop shoulder throw: the same entry as seoi nage, but the thrower falls to one or both knees
under you. It works best on an opponent who is bent forward, which is the posture many jiu-jitsu
players take to stop a double leg.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/seoi-otoshi)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/vu1TMVNnq34)

## Uchi mata {#uchi-mata}
@also uchimata, inner thigh throw

The inner thigh throw. The thrower breaks your balance forward and lifts your inner thigh with the
back of their leg. One of the most common throws in competition judo.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/uchi-mata)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/iUpSu5J-bgw)

## De ashi harai {#de-ashi-harai}
@also de ashi barai, forward foot sweep

The forward foot sweep. Timed for the moment you step and put your weight on that foot, so it takes
almost no force.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/de-ashi-harai)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/4BUUvqxi_Kk)

## Morote gari {#morote-gari}
@also double leg, double legs, double leg takedown

The two-hand reap: wrestling's double leg. It's banned in modern judo competition, so a judoka who
grips well may never have had to defend one.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/morote-gari)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/BHLQS4K85bs)

## Kuchiki taoshi {#kuchiki-taoshi}
@also single leg, single leg takedown

The single-hand drop: grab one leg, lift it and drive the opponent backwards. The judo relative of a
single leg takedown.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/kuchiki-taoshi)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/ZNL47q1aJNY)

## Kesa gatame {#kesa-gatame}
@also kesa, scarf hold

The scarf hold: sitting beside you, your arm trapped under theirs and their other arm around your
head. Escapes are slow and technical; the escapes page covers the classic ones.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/kesa-gatame)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/NDaQuJOFBYk)
- [Escapes](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/kesa-gatame:escapes)

## Yoko shiho gatame {#yoko-shiho-gatame}
@also side control, side mount

The side four-quarters hold, jiu-jitsu's side control: chest to chest across the opponent's body.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/yoko-shiho-gatame)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/TT7XJVSEQxA)
- [Escapes](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/yoko-shiho-gatame:escapes)

## Tate shiho gatame {#tate-shiho-gatame}
@also mount, full mount

The vertical four-quarters hold: lying on top of the opponent, straddling them, which is
jiu-jitsu's mount.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/tate-shiho-gatame)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/55-rFmBx53g)
- [Escapes](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/tate-shiho-gatame:escapes)

## Juji gatame {#juji-gatame}
@also armbar, armbars, arm bar, ude hishigi juji gatame

The cross armlock, jiu-jitsu's armbar: the arm held straight across the hips with the legs across the
chest, the elbow over the hips. The Kodokan's full name for it is *ude hishigi juji gatame*.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/ude-hishigi-juji-gatame)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/OWgSOlCuMXw)

## Ude garami {#ude-garami}
@also kimura, americana, figure four armlock

The entangled armlock: a figure-four grip on the wrist that bends the arm and turns the shoulder.
Turned one way it's the jiu-jitsu kimura, the other way the americana.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/ude-garami)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/AIlTvZb4RlE)

## Sankaku jime {#sankaku-jime}
@also triangle, triangle choke

The triangle choke: the neck and one arm trapped inside the legs, one foot locked behind the
other knee.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/sankaku-jime)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/lq1CUBRAm7s)

## Kata gatame {#kata-gatame}
@also arm triangle, arm triangle choke, head and arm choke

The shoulder hold. Judo lists it as a pin, but squeezing the opponent's arm against their own neck
makes it jiu-jitsu's arm triangle choke.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/kata-gatame)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/zQR3IOXxO_Q)

## Hadaka jime {#hadaka-jime}
@also rear naked choke, rnc

The naked strangle, jiu-jitsu's rear naked choke: from behind, forearm across the throat, no gi grips
needed.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/hadaka-jime)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/9f0n8jez7iA)
- [RNC as John Danaher · Bernardo Faria](https://youtu.be/t100UC79Jg4)

## Juji jime {#juji-jime}
@also cross collar, cross collar choke

The cross collar choke from the front, with the wrists crossed and a deep grip in each side of the
collar. The Kodokan names three versions by grip: palms up (*gyaku*), thumbs in (*nami*) and one of
each (*kata*).

- [Gyaku juji jime](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/gyaku-juji-jime)
- [Gyaku video](https://youtu.be/t3tQriIPdlI)
- [Nami juji jime](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/nami-juji-jime)
- [Nami video](https://youtu.be/k2cHry9HByQ)
- [Kata juji jime](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/kata-juji-jime)
- [Kata video](https://youtu.be/3VZVUAmiMD8)

## Okuri eri jime {#okuri-eri-jime}
@also sliding collar choke, bow and arrow, bow and arrow choke, clock choke

The sliding collar choke from behind: one hand deep in the collar, the other pulling the opposite
lapel. Jiu-jitsu's bow and arrow choke and the clock choke against a turtle are both built on this grip.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/okuri-eri-jime)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/EiqyoVcIAi8)

## Sode guruma jime {#sode-guruma-jime}
@also ezekiel, ezekiel choke

The sleeve wheel choke, jiu-jitsu's Ezekiel: one forearm across the throat, the other hand gripping
its own sleeve behind the neck. It works from inside someone's guard and even from bottom mount.

- [Technique page](https://judo.acmiyaguchi.me/waza/sode-guruma-jime)
- [Kodokan video](https://youtu.be/E3nvQzClcAU)

## Closed guard {#closed-guard}

Legs locked around the opponent's waist from your back. The guard of the old Gracie game, built on
breaking the opponent's posture and attacking the arms and neck.

- [The best closed guard · Roger Gracie](https://youtu.be/kPZh0ZZyZj0)

## Half guard {#half-guard}
@also knee shield

One of the opponent's legs trapped between yours. From the top, the bottom player's knee shield keeps
you away; from the bottom, the underhook decides who wins.

- [Passing half guard · John Danaher](https://youtu.be/Jz4oLDOHxLM)
- [Killing the knee shield · Yuri Simões](https://youtu.be/GFA1smN1UzQ)

## Lockdown {#lockdown}

10th Planet's half guard: the bottom player triple-wraps your trapped leg and stretches it to kill your
base, then uses the underhook to whip up into sweeps like the electric chair.

- [Unlock the lockdown · Bernardo Faria](https://youtu.be/bahZpShmZ80)

## Rubber guard {#rubber-guard}
@also mission control, new york, chill dog

Eddie Bravo's high guard: the bottom player clamps your head down with their own shin. Each stage has
a name (mission control, New York, chill dog) and the omoplata waits at the end of the chain.

- [Mastering the rubber guard · Eddie Bravo](https://youtu.be/RJ7Iutti8cY)

## The truck {#the-truck}
@also truck

A 10th Planet back-attack position: a leg triangle on one of your legs from your side. In no-gi it
leads to the twister and calf slicers; under this pack's rules it's a back-take machine.

- [Defending the truck](https://youtu.be/i6WiJXlRzaE)
- [Rubber guard and truck transitions · Richie Martinez and Geo Martinez](https://youtu.be/_fyB5b041MQ)

## Omoplata {#omoplata}
@also omoplatas

A shoulder lock with the legs: the opponent's arm is trapped against your hip and you sit up and turn
away. One of Bernardo Faria's specialities.

- [The omoplata · Bernardo Faria](https://youtu.be/ObMEoLogpcw)
- [Two omoplata escapes · Bernardo Faria](https://youtu.be/M0AF2_EZXDs)

## Spider guard {#spider-guard}

A gi open guard: sleeve grips with the feet in the opponent's biceps, holding them at the end of two
struts. It's only as strong as the sleeve grips.

- [Passing spider guard · Samuel Nagai](https://youtu.be/IiQhyeU2iZI)
- [Killing the spider guard · Guilherme Mendes](https://youtu.be/DIKlvvaW6PM)

## Hip escape {#hip-escape}
@also shrimp, shrimps, shrimping

Turning on your side and driving your hips away to make space: the first movement every jiu-jitsu
class drills, and the base of guard recovery.

- [The biggest mistake in the hip escape · Bernardo Faria](https://youtu.be/apizQSCXLwo)
- [An unstoppable hip escape · Henry Akins](https://youtu.be/4fyMiLey6rI)

## Upa {#upa}
@also bridge and roll, trap and roll

The bridge-and-roll mount escape: trap an arm and the foot on the same side, then bridge up and over
that shoulder.

- [The perfect upa · Henry Akins](https://youtu.be/RiIBK-moBdE)

## Tripod sweep {#tripod-sweep}

An open-guard sweep against a standing opponent: one foot on the hip, one hooking behind the heel,
then push and pull them over backwards.

- [Hidden details in the tripod sweep · Gordon Ryan](https://youtu.be/iGhMeN3TUPM)

## Butterfly guard {#butterfly-guard}

Seated guard with both feet hooked inside the opponent's thighs, made to lift and sweep.

- [Butterfly guard principles · Marcelo Garcia](https://youtu.be/0WG1MYvgXAM)

## Crossface {#crossface}
@also cross face

An arm across the opponent's face, turning their head away from you. In the gi, grabbing their far
lapel behind the head makes it far tighter.

- [Lapel crossface · John Danaher](https://youtu.be/eVU-T73fsWs)

## Back control {#back-control}
@also seatbelt, back take

Behind the opponent with a seatbelt grip (one arm over the shoulder, one under the armpit) and, for
the 4 points, both hooks in.

- [Controlling and attacking the back · Marcelo Garcia](https://youtu.be/-Htqm0ACI4U)
- [Back control concepts · Felipe Pena](https://youtu.be/ArB48LddQeI)

## Turtle {#turtle}
@also turtling, turtled

On knees and elbows, back to the opponent. A wrestler's position; against a judoka hunting chokes,
it's dangerous.

- [Understanding turtle · Gordon Ryan](https://youtu.be/A8JVwd_OoSY)
- [Escaping the seatbelt from turtle · Josh Barnett](https://youtu.be/kXlIvvCxumo)

## Straight ankle lock {#straight-ankle-lock}
@also ankle lock, straight foot lock

The one leg lock legal at every adult belt under these rules: the foot trapped in the armpit, the blade
of the forearm under the Achilles tendon.

- [The straight foot lock · Luiz Panza](https://youtu.be/Y5DrRj2GYcQ)
- [Straight foot lock escape · Gordon Ryan](https://youtu.be/lQ7EW_dPykM)
