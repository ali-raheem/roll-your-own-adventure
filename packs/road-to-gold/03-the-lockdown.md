While you warm up for the final, he's sitting on the edge of the mat in full lotus, chatting with his
teammates. At one point he reaches back, pulls his own foot up behind his head, and keeps talking like
nothing is happening.

In the semifinal you heard him narrate his own guard out loud: *"[[Mission control]]... New York... chill
dog..."* His opponent had no idea what any of it meant. Then he tapped.

Coach Teo watches him for a long moment. "He's going to be weird," he says. "Don't get weird with him.
He's wearing a gi. Make him *feel* it."

## Gi Grips {#l-footsweep}
@position Half guard · top

You take a deep grip on his left lapel with your right hand, his right sleeve with your left. He doesn't
fight either grip. He just tries to snap your head down with his free hand, the way he'd use a collar
tie in no-gi, while you're holding the handles of his jacket.

He steps in. You remember Darek's foot sweeps: you time his step and sweep his foot as the weight lands
on it. He goes down on his back. It's clean enough that the referee's two fingers go up.

But as he falls he's already whipping his legs around your right leg and locking them. You land on top of
something that feels like a leg-shaped bear trap.

@points you 2 Takedown
@learn GI-BLIND | Jax doesn't fight gi grips at all. He let you have a deep lapel grip for free: pure no-gi habits.

## The Double Leg {#l-double}
@position Half guard · top

## The Lockdown {#l-lockdown}
@position Lockdown half guard · top

He's on his side beneath you, both legs figure-foured around your right leg, and he's stretching it
backward so your knee is straight and your base is miserable. His right arm is deep under your left
armpit, an underhook, and his head is buried in your chest.

"You ever seen this before?" he asks, conversationally.

:::coach
The [[lockdown]] traps your leg to kill your base and stops the pass. The underhook is the other half: with
it, he can *whip up* for sweeps. Beat the underhook and you beat the lockdown.
:::

- [Posture up tall and rip your trapped leg free](#l-yank)

- [Flatten him: win the underhook, crossface with a grip on his lapel, and drive him onto his back](#l-flat)

## Reaching Back {#l-echair}
@position Half guard · bottom

You reach back with your right hand to peel his feet off your leg. It means you've taken a hand off him,
and all your weight is leaning away from his underhook.

He *whips up*: comes up on his elbow, underhook driving into your armpit, and grabs your far leg with his
free hand. With your trapped leg still locked, he stretches you apart like a wishbone. It's the
**[[electric chair]]**. You tip over onto your back and he comes up on top of you.

@points them 2 Sweep
@learn Reach back to deal with Jax's [[lockdown]] feet and you give him the whip-up and the electric chair sweep. Deal with the underhook first.

:::coach
From the electric chair, a lot of no-gi players keep stretching the hips for a submission. Today he takes
the two points and the top position.
:::

## Into the Truck {#l-truck-entry}

You've never been here before, but you've heard its name.

## The Truck {#l-truck}
@position The truck

He's behind and beside you, one leg locked around your leg, one hand reaching for your far arm. It's the
most 10th Planet position there is.

:::rule
In no-gi submission grappling [[the truck]] leads to the *twister*, a neck-and-spine crank, and to calf
slicers. At this event the twister is illegal (a spinal lock without a choke), and calf slicers are
for brown and black belts only. So here, the truck is a back-take machine.
:::

- [Turn away from him onto your belly to escape](#l-truck-back)
- [Sit back into him, keep your hips heavy, and fight to turn in and face him](#l-truck-escape)

## Escaping the Back {#l-back-escape}
@clock 1:50
@position Lockdown half guard · top

Both your hands peel the choking arm down off your neck. You scoot your hips away and let your
shoulders slide down to the mat on the side of his top arm, so it's trapped under your weight and can't
choke. Then you push his bottom hook off your thigh, kick your leg free and turn in to face him.

You end up on top in his [[half guard]]. He locks the [[lockdown]] again. But you're down on points and the

:::coach

other way and you fall straight into it.
:::

## New York, Chill Dog {#l-newyork}
@position Rubber guard · top

You pull your arm free. That's what he wanted: now your arm is on the outside of his leg. His shin
slides over your biceps to pin it ("New York"), then he pushes your head away and swings his leg over

[[Omoplata]]. You tap.

@lose Submission: omoplata from rubber guard

## Standing Out {#l-rubber-stand}

@learn Standing up kills Jax's rubber guard, but it hands him your legs. Be ready for the [[ankle lock]].

## Straight Ankle Lock {#l-ankle}
@position Ashi garami · defending

Your right foot is jammed under his armpit, his forearm blade cutting in just above your heel. One of his
legs is wrapped around your thigh, the other foot is on your hip. He grips his own lapel to lock it all
down and starts to arch.

:::rule
The [[straight ankle lock]] is legal at every adult belt. Heel hooks, toe holds and knee bars are not, at
blue and purple, which is why he's gone for this one.
:::

- [Point your toes and yank your foot out](#l-ankle-tap)

## Ankle Lock {#l-ankle-tap}
@position Ashi garami · defending

You point your toes and pull. Pointing your toes is exactly what the [[ankle lock]] wants: it gives his
forearm the perfect shelf under your Achilles. He arches his hips and your ankle and foot light up with
pain.

You tap fast. You'll need that ankle for the drive home.

@lose Submission: straight ankle lock
@learn Never point your toes in Jax's ankle lock. Boot the foot, get deep, sit on him.

## Surviving the Ankle Lock {#l-ankle-escape}

back. His forearm slides up your calf where it can't do anything. You drop your weight onto his hips and

He immediately wraps the leg he was attacking. [[Lockdown]] again.

@learn Jax goes to the [[ankle lock]] whenever you stand in his guard or pull guard on him. Boot the foot, get deep, sit on him.

## Ezekiel {#l-ezekiel}
@position Lockdown half guard · top, flat

You remember how he didn't fight a single grip standing up. Your [[crossface]] arm slides all the way behind
his neck, and your hand grips inside your own opposite sleeve. Your other hand, knife-edged, slides
across his throat.

He doesn't recognise it. He's still thinking about his [[lockdown]].

You squeeze. His eyes widen, and then he taps your back, fast, and mutters something rude and
admiring.

@win submission: Ezekiel choke from half guard

:::coach
The [[Ezekiel]] (*sode guruma jime*) uses your own sleeve as the handle. A no-gi specialist has spent years
never having to worry about your sleeves. You made him wear the gi, and then you made the gi matter.
:::
